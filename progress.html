Dates and data coverage

Gains are the difference between each player’s first and last valid WOM observations inside the selected window, ending at the group’s latest saved update. The rankings, table and graph use those same counters. WOM gain-summary endpoints are included when they have saved dates and values.

Shorter history is shown with its actual dates. A missing comparison stays unknown, while zero means two observations confirm no gain. A decreasing counter restarts the comparison at that correction. Lines connect observed values across gaps; no daily rates or boundary values are estimated.

Stat Progress measures the recent period. Time Records finds the best run anywhere in the saved history. Opening either page uses saved data; refresh Levels & KC manually to update it.
