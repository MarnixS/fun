Stats milestones via WOM

Recorded skill-level, XP, boss-KC and account milestones. Highlights include ordinary serious progression such as 70–95 skill landmarks and 25–250 KC steps, rather than only extreme endgame thresholds.

Drops / item unlocks via Temple

Collection Log additions recorded after a member first synced the Collection Log through Temple. These are item events, not WOM achievements.

Player-set goals

Members can publish what they are currently working toward so everyone in the group can see it. Tracker-derived suggestions remain separate below.

What are you working toward?

Set a goal for your account and make it visible to the whole group. The prompt is intentionally low-key: use it when you actually have something you want the others to know about.

Suggested tracker goals

Nearby milestones inferred from saved WOM stats and Collection Log data. These are suggestions from the tracker, not goals chosen by the player.
