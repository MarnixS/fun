# Chronicle NPC images

Downloaded from the Old School RuneScape Wiki on 13 October 2026. `sources.json`
records each original file page, download URL, original Wiki SHA-1, local SHA-256
and dimensions, plus the NPC artwork assigned to each WOM boss metric.

Small Wiki icons are retained where they depict the NPC. Other encounters use
the Wiki's transparent NPC rendering at a maximum of 64 pixels per dimension.
Raid modes share their final boss; Royal Titans and Moons of Peril use their
individual NPCs. Images are served locally and never fetched from the Wiki by
visitors. The site retains its existing Jagex fan-content notice.
