A clearer overview and easier comparisons

  • Six linked overview tiles show exact group XP, combined levels, log totals, latest achievements and the weekly XP leader.
  • Added KC comparison Beta and clickable player goals on the overview. Levels is now Stats; FAQ and this bulletin live under Other.
