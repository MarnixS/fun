24 October 2026

XP comparisons stay on the page; gear assemblies respect their parts

  • Select a stat to display its player comparison and graph below the stat menu. Periods, graph views and player filters remain accessible, with the chosen stat highlighted in the grid.
  • Gear details show the assembly recipe. Masori fortification consumes Armadyl armour through its plate yield; Voidwaker and other assemblies consume a complete set of parts. Bludgeon, halberd, axe and Brimstone ring pieces must be owned together by one crafter before the finished item can be shared.
24 October 2026

One finite gear pool, up to five boss loadouts

  • Team Gear Planner is available under Experimental. Boss-specific weapon priorities, phase switches and special attacks cover every encounter currently tracked by the site, including raid variants and skilling activities.
  • All five members supply tradeable gear. Reorder who receives scarce upgrades; personal equipment remains with its owner. Main weapons are assigned before switches, and the same player can reuse their equipment across phases.
  • Potential crafts consume their ingredients: Masori fortification, Torva repairs, Bowfa/crystal armour, zenyte jewellery and other upgrades share a finite resource ledger. Dismantled armour cannot also equip another player.
  • Adjust remaining stock to account for losses, consumed items or completed crafts. The log estimates lifetime acquisitions, and the planner distinguishes logged gear, potential crafts, confirmed stock and optional quest/shop assumptions. It uses researched priorities rather than calculated DPS.
