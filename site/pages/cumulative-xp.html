How the levels are calculated

Combined XP uses each selected account’s latest saved WOM skill values. The overall XP field is excluded to avoid counting XP twice. Select all skills for the complete account, or choose any combination.

Game levels stop at 99. Equivalent levels continue the standard OSRS experience curve beyond 99, including beyond 126 or 200 million pooled XP. These theoretical levels are a comparison, not an attainable in-game account. Merged total level adds the per-skill levels; pooled level treats all selected XP as one skill.

Unknown XP is shown as unknown and excluded from sums. Account snapshots may have different dates; the contribution table lists those dates.
