How these records are proved

We search every interval between saved WOM counters, including dated endpoints from gain summaries. Windows are rolling limits of 1, 7, 30, 90, 180 and 365 days. Total XP and total level are stats alongside the individual skills.

A long interval also proves something about shorter records. For example, 6,569,431 XP over 83.37 days fits into three runs of at most 30 days: at least one gained 2,189,811 XP or more. We apply this bound to every interval, including intervals longer than a year, for every player, metric and window. Integer XP, levels and kills round up. No XP is interpolated and no new observation dates are invented.

Each value is a proven minimum (≥), not a claim that unsaved gains were zero. If the strongest proof spans longer than the chosen window, its exact record dates are unknown; the evidence panel shows the real source interval instead. Otherwise, we show the observed supporting run. Possible ranges show what the gaps could contain within the saved history. Times before or after that history remain unknown.

Rows are sorted by their proven minimum. Possible ranks account for overlapping ranges; tied exact values share a rank. A dash means insufficient history, not zero. Unknown metrics and unranked zero-KC baselines are excluded. Decreasing counters split the history and leave an unknown upper bound.

The page uses the group’s saved WOM history. A manual Levels & KC refresh updates it for everyone; opening this page does not start a WOM update.
