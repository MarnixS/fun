How these records are deduced

For each metric, we search every pair of saved WOM snapshots that fits inside the selected window and keep the largest increase. These are rolling windows: 1 and 7 days, then 30, 90, 180 and 365 days. Shorter observed runs can set a record within a longer window.

Snapshot dates are irregular. Gains between the displayed endpoints are confirmed; the true best day or week could be higher during an unsaved gap. We do not interpolate XP or divide a long gap into invented daily gains. Rankings compare the best observed gains under the same window limit, rather than guaranteeing an all-time WOM record. Players with more history have more opportunities to set a recorded best.

Unknown metrics and unranked zero-KC boss baselines are excluded. A decreasing counter starts a new comparison segment, so corrections cannot inflate a record. Total XP and total level use WOM’s overall counters. Tied gains share a rank; missing records are not ranked as zero.

The page uses the group’s saved WOM history. A manual Levels & KC refresh updates it for everyone; opening this page does not start a WOM update.
