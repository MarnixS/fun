What this experiment measures

Every non-empty combination of the selected members is evaluated using the same saved WOM and Temple data. Collection Log coverage counts distinct obtained item IDs; duplicate quantities are preserved separately. Boss coverage counts distinct boss metrics with positive KC, with raid modes kept separate.

XP is combined across skills. The merged game total caps each skill at level 99. It is a virtual merged account, rather than the sum of the players’ individual total levels.

Fair credit is the exact average marginal contribution across every joining order, also called a Shapley value. For a shared log item, each owner receives an equal share of that item’s credit. The credits add up to the group total without double counting. Coverage targets find every smallest complete team that reaches the threshold.

These are alternate combinations of saved achievements, not predictions of drops or claims about what players would have done without each other. Missing logs and stats remain unknown. Clicking “Use this team” updates the shared member filter; exploring tiles does not. Experiment links restore the chosen team, comparison metric and coverage target within the current member pool.
