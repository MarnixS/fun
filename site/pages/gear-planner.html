    How recommendations and supply estimates work

    Research-based priorities, reviewed 24 October 2026. The OSRS Wiki combat engine calculates DPS for each player’s saved levels, assigned equipment and selected target. Defaults compare available weapons and improve armour choices by DPS, including complete crystal, void, Inquisitor and Blood Moon sets. Logged owners get first use across their main setup and phase switches. Unused equipment and spare copies pass to teammates in the displayed loan priority. Non-log personal rewards, such as an MA2 cape, can be confirmed for each account under Add non-log gear. This searches supported equipment combinations rather than every possible team allocation. Movement, mechanics, supplies and practical strategy can change the best choice.

    A Collection Log is lifetime acquisition history. It cannot prove current bank stock, losses, transfers or completed crafts. Adjust stock to correct these estimates. Personal gear stays with its owner; charged/cosmetic aliases do not create extra items. Potential crafts also need the quest, skill, currency and charge supplies stated on the item. Crafting requirements are shown rather than inferred; the level check covers listed equipment requirements, not every quest or unlock.

    Masori and Torva: Armadyl helmet/chestplate/chainskirt yield 1/4/3 plates. Masori mask/body/chaps need 1/4/3 plates plus the base piece. A full fortified set costs eight plates; unfortified Masori has the same ranged offence. Bandos chestplate/tassets yield 3/2 components; Torva helm/body/legs need 1/2/2 components. The ledger removes each donor piece exactly once.

    Assembled gear: one Voidwaker blade, hilt and gem make one weapon; five Venator shards make one bow; two burning claws make one weapon. Bludgeon, Noxious halberd, Brimstone ring and Soulreaper axe pieces must form a complete set on one crafter because their components are untradeable. Finished weapons and rings can be shared. Godswords compete for the same three-shard blade budget; heavy and light ballistae share limbs, springs and monkey tails.

    Solo encounters: multiple selected players represent separate simultaneous runs. Gauntlet uses its own equipment and does not consume group stock. Skilling presets use tools/outfits. Unverified new encounters are marked explicitly.

    Sources: Masori · Torva · Crystal equipment · Confliction · Avernic treads. The selected encounter’s strategy is linked above its loadouts.

    Choose equipment

    Green is available for this player after reserving other players’ equipment. Orange is acquired but allocated, consumed or personal to another player. Red has no available item or complete crafting recipe. Selecting orange or red creates a labelled hypothetical slot.

    Available equipment & ingredients

    Blank uses the log estimate. Enter 0 for lost or consumed items, or a confirmed remaining quantity. For finished crafts, inferred ingredients are reserved automatically. An explicit ingredient override means its remaining stock after crafting.

    Choose the account that actually owns a personal reward, such as an MA2 cape. Enter its remaining quantity; it will immediately become available to that account. Changes are saved in this browser.
